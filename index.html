About David Joel Schools

Our Background

David Joel School is a co-educational Primary and Secondary School founded on the quest for standard, qualitative and functional education.

The School was founded by seasoned education officers, supported by qualified, experienced and seasoned teachers who have vast knowledge in Sciences, Humanities, Commercial/Business Studies, Social Sciences and English Language.

The Proprietor and Proprietress taught in notable international schools such as Government College, Ibadan, Queen's School, Ibadan, and School of Science. They have helped produce excellent students who have progressed into higher institutions both at home and abroad.

Our Mandate

Our mandate is to develop the total child through teaching and training and to create a platform that produces mentally productive growing men and women who, by virtue of their training, become leaders of institutions and nations, providing solutions to the needs of mankind.

Our Vision

To be ranked among the best total education providers where academic and moral excellence is pursued in the most conducive environment, using modern and suitable educational resources and facilities.

Our Mission

We seek to produce men and women who acknowledge righteousness, believe in hard work, fear God and uphold respect for oneself and others, striving to be the best among their peers.

Godliness Diligence Fear of God Integrity
