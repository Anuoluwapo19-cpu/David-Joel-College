Our Background

David Joel School is a co-educational Primary and Secondary School founded on the quest for standard, quality and functional education.

The school was founded by experienced education officers with qualified and experienced teachers who have vast knowledge in Sciences, Humanities, Commercial/Business Studies and English Language.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Mandate, Vision & Mission

Our Mandate

Our mandate is to develop the total child through teaching and training and to create a platform that produces mentally productive growing men and women who by virtue of their training become leaders of institutions and nations, providing solutions to the needs of mankind.

Our Vision

To be ranked among the best total education providers where academic and moral excellence is pursued in a most conducive environment using modern and suitable educational resources and facilities.

Our Mission

We seek to produce men and women who acknowledge righteousness, believe in hard work, fear God and uphold respect for oneself and others, striving to be the best among their peers.
