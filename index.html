WHO WE ARE

About David Joel Model College

Welcome to David Joel Model College

David Joel Model College is an educational institution committed to providing students with an environment where learning, character development and personal growth can thrive.

The school has grown over the years and has different branches serving students and families in different communities.

In 2026, the school celebrated its 24th Anniversary, marking an important milestone in its journey.

School Pledge

a. I will be courageous and not be dismayed in the face of odds.

b. I will never surrender to discouragement or despair, nor matter the seeming obstacle that may confront me.

c. I will take new faith and resolution from the knowledge that all successful men and women had to fight defect, depression and adversity.

d. I will try again and again to accomplish what I desire.

e. I will believe that all things will work out for me if I hang on to the end.

f. I will not permit anyone or any circumstance to intimidate me or determine from my goals.

g. I will never give up as long as I know I am in God's will.
